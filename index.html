Profile summary

My journey

My interest in Information Technology began when I was around 15 years old. Although I did not yet see it as a career path, my mother recognised my talent and encouraged me to explore it. In 2019, I enrolled in Civil Engineering, but quickly realised that building and construction were not the right fit for me. The turning point came in 2020, when I worked as a Junior IT Technician, configuring and rolling out smartwatches for clients. It was there that I discovered my true passion for technology and committed myself to a career in IT.

About me

I am the founder of GTECH, a solutions-driven business focused on delivering technology, branding, and support services to small and growing enterprises. My mission is to help businesses build a professional identity and improve operational efficiency through practical, strategic technology solutions.
